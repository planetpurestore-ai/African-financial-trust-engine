Verify a transaction

Submit an invoice with supporting purchase order, contract and/or payment evidence. The engine returns a decision, score, risk assessment and audit hash.

Documents

Upload invoices and supporting documents directly to the engine. PDF text extraction and configured image OCR are processed server-side.

Attach document to transaction

Upload a document, then link it to a transaction. The transaction ID is filled automatically after verification, or you can paste an existing ID.

Compare transaction verification results and supporting evidence. The verification engine performs supplier, buyer, amount, currency, commercial-evidence and payment-evidence checks.

Account & connections

Your account session is authenticated and organization-scoped. External bank and mobile-money sources require provider credentials and partnerships; unconfigured sources are not represented as live.

Sign in to view account details.

Financial data connections

Connectors are designed to be added provider by provider. Choose one bank, mobile-money operator, or payment provider, then follow the implementation checklist. A source remains disconnected until its credentials, permissions, authenticated data flow, and tests are complete.

No external financial source is assumed to be connected. Check status to see the provider catalog and required next steps.
